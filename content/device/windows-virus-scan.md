## Windows Virus Scan: How to Check Your PC Without Stress

A Windows virus scan is one of the easiest ways to keep your computer safe. You do not need special skills or paid software. Windows already has built-in tools that can find and remove many threats before they cause problems.

This page shows you how to scan your PC in a simple, clear way.

## Why Running a Virus Scan Matters

Viruses and other bad software can slow down your computer, steal your information, or show unwanted ads.

Common signs include:

- Your PC feels slow
- Strange pop-ups appear
- Programs open by themselves

<Warning>If your computer acts strangely, run a virus scan right away.</Warning>

Scanning early can stop small problems from becoming big ones.

## Use Windows’ Built-In Security Tool

Windows comes with a free security tool called Windows Security. It can scan your computer and block many threats.

To find it:

- Click the Start menu
- Search for “Windows Security”
- Open the app

<Tip>You do not need to install anything extra to get basic protection.</Tip>

This tool works quietly in the background, but you can also run scans manually.

## How to Run a Windows Virus Scan

Running a scan only takes a few minutes.

Follow these steps:

- Open Windows Security
- Click Virus & threat protection
- Click Quick scan

Windows will start checking your computer for problems.

<Info>A quick scan checks the most common places where viruses hide.</Info>

If you want a deeper check, choose Full scan instead.

## What to Do If a Threat Is Found

If Windows finds something, it will tell you what to do.

Usually, you can:

- Remove the threat
- Quarantine it
- Restart your computer

<DoDont doText="Let Windows remove or block any threats it finds." dontText="Ignore warnings or close them without reading." />

Following the instructions keeps your PC safe.

## How Often Should You Scan?

You do not need to scan every day, but regular checks help.

A good habit is:

- Run a quick scan once a week
- Run a full scan if something feels wrong

<Tip>Set a reminder so you do not forget.</Tip>

Regular scans catch problems early.

## Avoid Fake Virus Warnings

Some websites show fake alerts that claim your PC is infected. They try to scare you into paying money or calling a number.

Real Windows virus scans:

- Do not ask for payment
- Do not show phone numbers
- Do not lock your screen

<Warning>If a pop-up asks you to call someone to fix a virus, close the browser.</Warning>

Only trust scans from Windows Security or trusted software.

## Keep Your Protection Working

A Windows virus scan works best when everything is up to date.

Make sure to:

- Update Windows
- Keep Windows Security turned on
- Restart your PC when updates finish

<Info>Updates fix security holes that viruses use to get in.</Info>

Small updates make a big difference.
